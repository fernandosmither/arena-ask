Applies from the next question. After a switch, each chapter starts a new chat.

Before you rely on it

Anything on learn.arena.education (the lesson text, the page's scripts, its saved chat) travels with your question, so it can steer the answer, and the page can read the answer. In full mode that answer can draw on your memory and past chats. Actions such as sending email are stopped and handed to you to approve, but may already have started (Claude) or run (ChatGPT, whose Stop was seen to land about 2 seconds late). Only approve actions you asked for.
